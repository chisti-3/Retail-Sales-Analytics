# Retail Sales Analytics

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates an end-to-end Retail Sales Analytics solution built using Microsoft Excel.

The objective is to design an interactive business dashboard that enables management to monitor sales performance, customer behavior, product profitability, and regional trends across India.

---

## Business Problem

ElectroMart India Pvt. Ltd. operates retail stores across multiple Indian states.

The company generates thousands of sales transactions every month.

Management currently relies on manually prepared Excel reports from different regional offices.

This results in:

- Delayed reporting
- Inconsistent KPIs
- Limited visibility into sales performance
- Difficulty tracking customer trends
- Time-consuming manual analysis

---

## Project Objective

Develop an interactive Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard that enables stakeholders to:

- Monitor revenue and profit
- Analyze regional sales
- Identify top-performing products
- Track customer purchasing behavior
- Compare sales across different periods
- Support business decision-making
